Contact form

When you send the contact form, your name, email, website, message and the options you choose are delivered to my email through Formspree, a form-handling service. I use them only to reply to you and to discuss possible work, and I delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

Analytics cookies

If you accept cookies, the site loads Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes data such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. Advertising features and data sharing with Google for other purposes are switched off. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you by emailing me. You can also lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).
